# 16 — Emotional Intelligence | Learning Lab

An evidence-aware, responsive, standalone educational app for emotional awareness, emotion science, regulation, empathy, social relationships, and human flourishing. Part of the Central Hub learning repository series.

## Modules

- **Explore:** progress overview and domain navigation.
- **Theory Library:** 38 searchable frameworks across 10 domains, with central ideas, practices, limitations and reading leads.
- **Concept Map:** domain-filtered navigation across related frameworks.
- **Emotion Atlas:** emotion labeling, valence and activation map, contextual observations and JSON export.
- **EI Simulator (V2):** configure emotional triggers, perceived activation, cognitive appraisals, regulation strategies and behavioral responses; compare interpretations, save/export experiments and navigate to the Regulation Lab.
- **Regulation Lab:** seven regulation strategies and saved action experiments.
- **Empathy Studio:** structured observation, perspective-taking, alternative hypotheses, clarification and boundaries.
- **Social Scenarios:** five applied situations with choices, contextual feedback, relevant theory links and learning history.
- **Compare:** side-by-side framework analysis.
- **Recall Lab:** 16 retrieval prompts with heuristic review intervals.
- **Study Path:** five guided phases.
- **Journal:** six reflection prompts with local persistence and export.

## Deploy

In GitHub, open **Settings → Pages** and configure **Deploy from a branch → main → /(root)**.

Expected Pages URL: https://obiwandac0-d.github.io/16-Emotional-Intelligence/

The application is self-contained in `index.html`, requires no build system or external dependencies, and stores personal learning data in browser localStorage. Data does not automatically sync between devices. Use the export controls to back up entries.

## Evidence and ethical boundaries

Emotional intelligence is not a single uncontested construct. Ability, trait and mixed competency models differ, and measurement validity remains debated. Emotion labels, bodily sensations and observed social cues are not diagnostic. Scenario feedback is illustrative rather than a validated assessment. Regulation strategies are context-dependent, and social conditions and unequal feeling rules matter. This is an educational application, not therapy or crisis care.

Foundational reading leads have not been independently verified as a systematic literature review.
